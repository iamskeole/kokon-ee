features from upstream. 0.12.1 (released 2026-11-11, 10 commits,
`f9b6971..bff35ca`) is the test case: small, feature-rich, and it touches
exactly the three areas where our fork diverges most.

## blockers

- **task-029 first** — the rebase runs on the new repo, after the
  identity pass (so the first public commits on the new repo are the
  credit, then the sync, in that order).
- The deploy window may touch the GPU (one image job for the owner's
  visual check of more-like-this) → the autonomy protocol applies to any
  engine-boot phase; the service-image swap itself is CPU-side.
- The live box config is schema 15 with `multiUser` on and the adopted
  schema-14 native-PE wiring (`config.json.pre028` is the PE rollback) —
  the rebase's config-schema changes must carry all of that; the box's
  config backup convention applies.
- (Watch) the 0.12.1 change set is small but touches `engine.rs` (2.9k
  lines of our divergence) — if the conflict surface in `engine.rs` turns
  out to be structural rather than local, stop and record it here before
  forcing it (the task-024 lesson: 13 stops, all understood).

## decisions

- **D-030-1 — Rebase the full tree (stock path still present).** That is
  what makes it the honest in-vivo test — "independence still lets us pull
  features from upstream" means the upstream tree as it is, including the
  stock path the fork keeps for now. The chaff cut (task-031) comes
  after, deleting the *final* shape of each file (0.12.1's version of the
  chaff, not the pre-0.12.1 one).
- **D-030-2 — (to confirm in-session) EE mode: the service never unloads
  a gateway-owned model.** The 0.12.1 idle-unload applies to the stock
  path only; for external pipelines the lifecycle skips it and the
  Settings control is hidden (the gateway's TTL — measured 120 s — is the
  authority, faster than his 10 minutes anyway). This closes the #52
  watch item with the recorded principle: *the service only ever points
  at entrypoints, API keys, and prompts* (PLAN §0 rule 3).
- **D-030-3 — (to confirm in-session) the output-folder composition:**
  single-user + toggle on = `<output>/<project title>/` (byte-identical to
  upstream's new behaviour); multi-user = `<output>/<name>/<project
  title>/`; toggle off = today's per-day folders; MCP/API = per-day (as
  upstream).
- **D-030-4 — (to confirm in-session) more-like-this in EE mode:** the
  job request carries `vary` through our existing gateway-client job
  body; if the stock sdcpp protocol expresses it as steps (0.5 → 4 of 8,
  0.7 → 6 of 8, per 0.12.1's "last half of Turbo's 8 steps"), the mapping
  is recorded with the measured step counts per pipeline (klein's step
  count may differ — per-pipeline mapping, not a constant tuned on one
  model).
- **D-030-5 — The site/ changes (version bumps, What's-new copy) are
  parked with site/** — it is upstream marketing, dead in our direction;
  task-031 deletes or tags it. Nothing in task-030 depends on it.
